# P4-17: the swap-check assumptions

Harness: `tests/integration/Tuning/SwapBench.php` (group `tuning`). It seeds `docs/fixtures/queue-2026-11-09.json` with the pinned clock (2026-11-09 14:00, America/Los_Angeles) and adds twenty synthetic scheduled posts over the next 90 days at four-day spacing (ten in their lane, ten out of it, through `Slots::insert()`; Wednesdays are skipped because the Wednesday lane accepts every category). Each candidate value is set through the `masthead_config` filter and the swap check is run once with `NullStats` and once with `FixtureStats` on `docs/fixtures/stats-sample.json`. Environment: wp-env, WordPress 7.1.4, PHP 8.5.12.

Every default is kept: no decision rule fired, so `src/Config.php` is unchanged and each "after" row equals its "before" row. `SwapCheckMantleTest` is unaffected.

## swap.lookahead_days

Findings in the run (fixture plus synthetic posts), wall time of one run, and whether the Mantle acceptance (`lane_mismatch` for parts 2 to 4 and the part 2 `cadence_break`) still passes. Rule: keep unless a run exceeds 2 s (then the largest measured value under it), and never go below the value at which the Mantle acceptance still passes.

| Row | lookahead_days | lane_mismatch | collision | cadence_break | better_candidate | seconds | Mantle |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| alternative | 30 | 3 | 2 | 0 | 1 | 0.011 | FAIL (part 2 is 35 days out) |
| before | 60 | 9 | 4 | 1 | 2 | 0.025 | pass |
| alternative | 92 | 12 | 6 | 1 | 3 | 0.030 | pass |
| after | 60 | 9 | 4 | 1 | 2 | 0.025 | pass, unchanged |

The counts with `NullStats` and with `FixtureStats` were identical at every value (the sample statistics do not change which findings exist).

## swap.min_score_delta

Rule: keep unless, with stats unavailable, a post in its own lane receives a `better_candidate` finding; then the smallest measured value at which none does.

| Row | min_score_delta | better_candidate (all) | in-lane posts with better_candidate (NullStats) | rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| alternative | 0.15 | 2 | 0 | |
| before | 0.25 | 2 | 0 | passes |
| alternative | 0.35 | 2 | 0 | |
| after | 0.25 | 2 | 0 | unchanged |

An in-lane post has a lane term of 1.0 and so cannot be beaten by any positive delta without statistics.

## swap.candidate_window_days

Rule: keep unless more than half of the in-lane synthetic posts receive `better_candidate` with fixture stats; then the largest measured value at which they do not.

| Row | candidate_window_days | better_candidate (all, fixture stats) | in-lane posts with better_candidate (fixture stats) | rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| alternative | 2 | 2 | 0 of 10 | |
| before | 3 | 2 | 0 of 10 | passes |
| alternative | 5 | 2 | 0 of 10 | |
| after | 3 | 2 | 0 of 10 | unchanged |

## swap.weights

For every in-lane synthetic post and every out-of-lane synthetic post, the out-of-lane post is scored on the in-lane post's date with fixture stats (technology 325 views and writing 210 are the only categories with traffic). Rule: keep the default unless an in-lane post is outscored on its own day by an out-of-lane post; then the first measured alternative where that does not happen.

| Row | weights (lane / category / weekday) | pairs where the out-of-lane post outscores the in-lane post |
|---|---|---|
| before | 0.5 / 0.3 / 0.2 | 0 of 90 |
| alternative | 0.6 / 0.2 / 0.2 | 0 of 90 |
| alternative | 0.4 / 0.4 / 0.2 | 0 of 90 |
| after | 0.5 / 0.3 / 0.2 | 0 of 90 (unchanged) |

`NOT MEASURED (needs real Jetpack Stats on the live site; human)`: the thresholds were judged on invented statistics and synthetic posts, so they say the defaults are not obviously wrong, not that they are right for the live blog.
