# P3-06: stats.cache_ttl

Harness: `tests/unit/Tuning/StatsCacheBench.php` (group `tuning`, excluded from the default unit run). It simulates one day against `Stats\Cache` with an in-memory transient store that honours expiry and a clock advanced through the `masthead_now` filter: one nightly warm at `cron.nightly_hour` (03:00), then one read of each of the three summary methods (`get_visits`, `get_top_posts`, `get_tags`) every 30 minutes from 06:00 to 22:00. It reports the upstream calls per day and the greatest age of a value served.

Decision rule: keep 21600 unless it makes more than 12 upstream calls per day or serves a value older than 6 hours; then choose the measured lifetime that satisfies both, or the one with the smaller greatest age if none does.

| Row | ttl (s) | upstream calls per day | greatest age served (h) | rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| before (SPEC default) | 21600 | 12 | 5.50 | passes (12 is not more than 12; 5.5 h is not more than 6 h) |
| alternative | 3600 | 54 | 0.50 | not needed |
| alternative | 86400 | 3 | 19.00 | not needed |
| after | 21600 | 12 | 5.50 | unchanged |

Decision: keep `stats.cache_ttl` at 21600. `src/Config.php` is unchanged.

Not measured: real upstream latency and Jetpack's own five-minute transient cache (`NOT MEASURED (needs a connected Jetpack site; human)`).
