# P1-16: calendar.max_posts_per_range

Harness: `tests/integration/Tuning/PlanRangeBench.php` (group `tuning`). For each cap it inserts that many `future` posts spread evenly over `calendar.max_range_days` (92) days from 2026-11-01 through `Slots::insert()`, sets the cap with the `masthead_config` filter, and records the median of five `PlanReader::range()` calls over the whole range and the process peak memory (wp-env, WordPress 7.1.4, PHP 8.5.12).

Decision rule: keep 500 unless its median exceeds one second or its peak memory exceeds 64 MB; in that case choose the largest measured cap that passes.

| Row | cap | posts returned | median ms | peak MB | rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| before (SPEC default) | 500 | 500 | 48.9 | 56.5 | passes (under 1 s and 64 MB) |
| alternative | 250 | 250 | 24.9 | 52.5 | not needed |
| alternative | 1000 | 1000 | 92.9 | 60.5 | not needed |
| after | 500 | 500 | 48.9 | 56.5 | unchanged |

Peak memory includes WordPress and the test library (about 50 MB baseline), so the marginal cost is roughly 8 MB per 1000 posts.

Decision: keep `calendar.max_posts_per_range` at 500. `src/Config.php` is unchanged.
